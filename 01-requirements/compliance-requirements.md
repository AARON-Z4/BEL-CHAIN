# Compliance Requirements

- Apply data minimization: no unnecessary personal or sensitive payloads on-chain.
- Apply role-based authorization and auditable approval.
- Maintain evidence for access, identity, ownership, custody, quality, and maintenance actions.
- Align deployment with applicable BEL, Ministry of Defence, Indian data-protection, procurement, and information-security requirements.
- Treat international mappings such as ISO 27001, NIST 800-53, NIST 800-171, and CMMC as reference frameworks unless contractually applicable.

# Proposed Solution

BEL-Chain is a hybrid, permissioned blockchain platform built around four modules:

- **BEL-DID:** decentralized identities and verifiable credentials for employees, suppliers, devices, systems, and assets.
- **BEL-RBAC/ABAC:** role and attribute-based authorization with least privilege.
- **BEL Asset Passport:** unique NFT-style, non-fungible asset records for physical and digital assets.
- **BEL Audit Chain:** immutable events for identity, permission, asset, quality, custody, and maintenance operations.

Confidential payloads remain encrypted in approved off-chain storage. The ledger retains identifiers, hashes, permission decisions, timestamps, signatures, and provenance references.

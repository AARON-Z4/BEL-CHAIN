# Scope

## In scope
- Permissioned blockchain network and chaincode.
- DID registration, credential issuance, revocation, and recovery.
- Role and attribute-based access decisions.
- Asset passport minting, ownership transfer, custody events, QC and maintenance references.
- Audit dashboard and reports.
- RFID/QR integration interfaces.

## Out of scope for MVP
- Storage of classified documents on-chain.
- Replacing existing ERP or HR systems.
- Real money payments or public cryptocurrency functions.
- Autonomous asset transfer without authorized approval.

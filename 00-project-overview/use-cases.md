# Use Cases

## UC-01: Create verified identity
An authorized administrator creates a DID for an employee or supplier and issues scoped credentials.

## UC-02: Assign access
A manager assigns a role and policy attributes such as unit, project, clearance, and validity period.

## UC-03: Mint asset passport
An authorized unit manager creates a unique asset passport linked to serial number, RFID/QR identifier, manufacturer DID, and document hashes.

## UC-04: Transfer custody
A supplier and BEL receiver confirm an asset handover; chaincode records the custody event.

## UC-05: Record quality evidence
A quality auditor uploads encrypted evidence off-chain and anchors its hash to the asset passport.

## UC-06: Verify audit history
An auditor filters events by asset, identity, project, unit, and time period, then generates a report.

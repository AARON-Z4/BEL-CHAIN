# Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest | Role in platform |
|---|---|---|
| BEL Corporate Administration | Governance and platform policy | Network/policy administration |
| BEL Manufacturing Units | Production assets and workflow | Asset creation and custody |
| Project Managers | Project-controlled access | Scoped approvals |
| Quality Teams | Certificate and inspection evidence | Audit and QC entries |
| Operators | Asset operation and maintenance | Maintenance/custody logs |
| Suppliers/Contractors | Controlled collaboration | Limited asset/document access |
| Defence Stakeholders | Provenance and audit visibility | Authorized read-only access |
| Security/IT Teams | Security and availability | Monitoring and incident response |

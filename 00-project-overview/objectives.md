# Objectives

- Establish cryptographically verifiable identities for authorized participants.
- Enforce RBAC and ABAC policies for sensitive resources.
- Create traceable, unique asset passports for components, documents, and equipment.
- Record asset ownership, custody, quality, and maintenance history.
- Produce tamper-evident audit evidence.
- Integrate with BEL ERP, RFID/QR, document management, and SIEM systems.
- Preserve data sovereignty and minimize on-chain sensitive data.

# Problem Statement

## Organization
Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL).

## Title
Blockchain-Based Secure Platform for Identity, Access Control, and Digital Asset Management.

## Problem
Centralized identity and access systems can create single points of failure, identity-theft exposure, unauthorized access risk, and difficult audits. Asset ownership, authenticity, quality evidence, and custody records may be fragmented across units, suppliers, ERP systems, spreadsheets, and document repositories.

## Required outcome
Build a decentralized, tamper-evident platform that links verified identities, controlled access rights, unique digital asset records, ownership/custody history, and immutable audit events.

# Design System

## Colors
- Navy: `#0C1F3A`
- Cyan: `#0096B6`
- Green: `#20825A`
- Orange: `#DC7D23`
- Background: `#F4F8FB`

## UI principles
- Defence-grade, uncluttered, data-first presentation.
- Role-aware navigation and actions.
- Clear badges for active, suspended, revoked, restricted, pending and approved states.
- Confirmation modal for privileged actions.
- Audit timeline and asset provenance timeline are first-class components.
- Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard navigation expectations.
